01 / About

A researcher rooted in Mangalore's academic community

Dr. P. Sridhara Acharya is an Associate Professor at the College of Computer Science and Information Science, Srinivas University, in Srinivas Nagar, Mukka, Surathkal — on the coastal belt of Mangalore, Karnataka. His scholarship centers on renewable energy resources, with particular attention to how solar and other renewable systems can be made more efficient and accessible in rural Indian settings.
