Dr. P. Sridhara Acharya — Multi-page Website

Files:
- index.html — Home
- about.html — About Me
- experience.html — Education & Experience
- research.html — Research & Publications
- teaching.html — Teaching
- achievements.html — Achievements
- contact.html — Contact

- sridhara-acharya.png — supplied profile photograph

Preview locally:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.

Publish online:
Upload all files together to a static hosting service or your institution's web server.
Keep the HTML files, CSS, and photograph in the same folder.

Research note:
The website links to Google Scholar, Vidwan and ORCID rather than hardcoding changeable citation metrics. A publication highlight is included based on the publicly indexed Srinivas Publications article.
