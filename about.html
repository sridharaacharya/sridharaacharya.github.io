About Me

An academic professional whose work spans computer science education, research, institutional service and Carnatic classical music.

Professional biography

Dr. P. Sridhara Acharya is a professor and computer science educator with teaching experience dating back to 1998. He has served in BCA and MCA education, held academic leadership roles, and contributed to university-level Boards of Studies and Boards of Examinations. He earned his Ph.D. in 2023 for research on the design and study of an electronic charge controller for optimal utilisation of solar energy.

Alongside his academic career, he is a Carnatic violinist who began learning at the age of eight. Since 1993, he has accompanied singers and Bharatanatyam artists, performed at cultural venues across India, and composed music for dance dramas and devotional compositions.
