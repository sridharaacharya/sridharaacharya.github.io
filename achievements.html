As an artist: Carnatic violin

Dr. Acharya began learning Carnatic violin in 1983 at the age of eight. His teachers and mentors include Sri Raviraj Upadhya, Late Narasimha Upadhya and Dr. Nagaraja Upadhya of Balagana Mela, Udupi. He completed Junior and Senior levels under their guidance and began accompanying singers and Bharatanatyam artists in 1993.

  • Vice President, Akhila Karnataka Makkala Sammelana, Ujire, 1987.
  • Participated in the South Zone Youth Festival at Gulbarga University, 1993.
  • Performed at DD Chandana, AIR Mangalore, TTD Tirupati, Kalidasa Hall Mumbai, and stages across India.
  • Composed music and wrote dance dramas including Sri Krishna Navarasa, Sri Ganesha Charithe, Ekalavya, Deepavali and Navarasa Krishna Bharatha Yaksha Nrutya.
  • Created Pushpanjali and Devaranama compositions and composed music for them.
